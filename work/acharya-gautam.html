Acharya Gautam - a 0-to-1 brand, booking website and local SEO build for an astrologer and Vastu consultant.

Turning a practising Vastu consultant and astrologer's word-of-mouth reputation into a brand, a researched local SEO strategy, and a custom-built website that takes real bookings and payments - live at acharyagautam.com. Research, design, development, testing and SEO, run end to end.

Summary

Mission

Acharya Gautam - Astrovaidya - is a Vastu consultant and astrologer whose practice had grown almost entirely through referrals. The brief was to give that reputation a front door: a brand that feels trustworthy rather than mystical for its own sake, a website that explains the services plainly, states its fees and actually takes a booking and a payment, and a local SEO strategy so the practice shows up for the searches that lead to a real consultation.

My contributions

I'm running this end to end: competitive and market research across 100+ Vastu and astrology consultants - local, city, state and national, including the top-ranked names, a brand-strategy review and positioning, the site's information architecture and UX design, and the full front-end and back-end build - a custom booking flow, a browser-side panchang (Hindu almanac) engine, payment links and automated confirmation emails. Then cross-device testing, a local SEO and content strategy, and a Reels/Shorts content calendar planned around the Panchang.

Live, and still growing

Research, brand, the full build and QA are done, and the site is live and taking bookings and payments. Local SEO - Google Business Profile, Search Console and locality pages - is the active phase, with results and the next round of screens to follow.

  • 01Vastu, not astrology, is the local SEO advantage. A chart reading doesn't care where the astrologer sits, so "astrologer near me" is a search Gautam competes in against every platform in India. A Vastu site visit is physically constrained - proximity is a genuine reason to choose him there, so local SEO leads with Vastu.
  • 02The Google Business Profile outranks the website. For "near me" searches, Google shows a map with listings above the organic results. A local competitor with 750+ reviews and a weak website still wins that spot. Nothing on the site fixes that - only a verified, active profile does.
  • 03Published prices are a differentiator, not a risk. Almost nobody in the local market states fees plainly. A fee ladder, a refund policy and a stated "what we will not do" (no outcome guarantees, no fear-led claims) do more for trust than another testimonial would.
  • 04A brand document is only useful if it agrees with itself. The strategy review caught a pricing ladder that contradicted the live site by 2.5x on its entry product, and a name spelling that didn't match the astrologer's own published certificates - both resolved before they cost a booking.

A brand deck and guidelines, built from the live site - not invented first.

Most brand decks come before the product and get ignored once it ships. This one came after: I audited what the live site already does well and turned it into the reference for everything built next - colour, logo, type, imagery, motion, accessibility and how the brand shows up on social, in one document.

  • Brand essence & positioning test
  • Colour tokens, measured for contrast
  • Logo clear space & misuse rules
  • Typography system - Fraunces / Inter
  • Imagery rules & banner sizes
  • Accessibility & motion guidelines
Built from the live site's own design system: every colour is a measured token with its contrast ratio against the page background (AA, 4.5:1 and up), not a mood-board guess. The logo rules cover clear space, minimum size, and four real ways to misuse it - stretched, rotated, given an effect, cropped to a badge - mistakes that take no bad intent, just a quick resize or a template default. Imagery has one test: "could a competitor have bought this exact picture? If yes, it's the wrong picture."

From research to a live product.

Not a template. A custom front end and back end, built and deployed by me, end to end.

  • Consultation booking & intake flow
  • Browser-side panchang engine
  • Payment links, no manual UPI
  • Automated confirmation emails
  • Mobile-first, responsive build
  • Automated price & layout checks
One detail worth sharing: the panchang engine computes sunrise, sunset, tithi, nakshatra and the choghadiya periods in the browser, with no API and no recurring cost - checked against published panchang data and an independently written reference implementation, and accurate to the minute on sunrise and sunset. Where a value can't be computed honestly, the site says "not calculated" rather than guess.

Testing, and SEO built in, not bolted on.

Two different kinds of discipline that both come down to the same thing: don't ship a guess.

  • QAReal devices find what desktop testing doesn't. A time-of-birth field worked perfectly in desktop Chrome and was completely stuck on a real phone - a WebKit quirk with no warning in the console. Fixed by replacing the native picker with a validated text field, and checked for the same pattern anywhere else it was reused.
  • QARules that check themselves. Prices and layout breakpoints live in more than one place by necessity (markup, scripts, copy). Small scripts run against the live files and fail loudly if any of them drift out of sync, instead of relying on remembering to check.
  • SEOSchema, not just copy. ProfessionalService, Person and FAQPage structured data, a title/meta pattern naming the service and the place on every page, and compressed images with descriptive alt text throughout.
  • SEONo invented numbers. Search volumes weren't available without a paid tool or a connected Search Console, so the keyword strategy is built on search intent, not guessed traffic figures - and says so.

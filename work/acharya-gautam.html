Acharya Gautam — a personal brand and website for an astrologer and Vastu consultant.

A current engagement: turning a practising astrologer's word-of-mouth reputation into a personal brand, a website that books consultations, and a festival-led content plan for Reels and Shorts. This page is being written as the work happens — sections will fill in as each phase ships.

Summary

Mission

Acharya Gautam — Astrovaidya — is a spiritual guide and Vastu consultant whose practice has grown almost entirely through referrals. The brief is to give that reputation a front door: a brand that feels trustworthy rather than mystical for its own sake, a website that explains the services plainly and makes booking a consultation easy, and a steady stream of short-form content tied to the Hindu festival calendar so the practice stays visible between consultations.

My contributions

I'm running this end to end: competitive and UX research across astrology and wellness sites, positioning and brand identity, the website's information architecture and design, and a content calendar for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts planned around the Panchang from September to December 2026. Later phases include affiliate-friendly evergreen content and the site build.

What the research is pointing at.

Early findings, subject to change as the brand work sharpens them.

  • Trust is the product. Most astrology sites lean on mysticism and urgency. The gap is a calm, credential-led presence that explains what a consultation actually involves.
  • Booking is the conversion. Visitors arrive from a Reel or a referral with one question: how do I talk to him? That path has to be one tap from anywhere.
  • Festivals set the rhythm. Interest spikes are predictable — Navratri, Diwali, Ganesh Chaturthi — so content and site features can be planned months ahead from the Panchang.
  • Evergreen pays the bills. Festival content brings reach; generic, always-relevant topics carry the affiliate products that make the channel sustainable.

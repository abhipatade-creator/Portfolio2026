Phishing Simulation Platform

LRN Technologies · Sept 2023 – Nov 2025

RoleUX Consultant — Outsourced
PlatformEnterprise Web SaaS
IndustryCybersecurity · Compliance
Project Type0 → 1 Product Design
Discovery & Research

The Problem

LRN's compliance training suite lacked a native phishing simulation capability. Security teams managed campaigns manually across disconnected tools — with no unified analytics, no reusable template library, and no mechanism to close the loop between a failed simulation and remediation training.

!No centralised platform for launching, managing, and tracking phishing simulations
!Campaign configuration required multiple disconnected tools and manual CSV exports
!No reusable template system — every campaign built from scratch by security teams
!Reporting buried in raw data exports with no actionable risk scoring or trend view
!No direct connection between simulation failures and LRN's remediation training modules

Objective

Design a purpose-built phishing simulation platform integrating natively into LRN’s compliance ecosystem — enabling security teams to launch campaigns, monitor real-time engagement, identify at-risk employees, and automatically assign remediation training.

Design Goals

✓Campaign creation in under 5 minutes for security admins
✓Real-time risk scores surfaced at individual and team level
✓Reusable template library reducing campaign setup overhead
✓Closed-loop: failure → training assignment automated
✓WCAG 2.1 AA accessible, GDPR-compliant data handling

LOTO · Permit-to-Work Web Platform · Outsourced

RoleUX Consultant — Outsourced
PlatformEnterprise Web App
IndustryIndustrial Safety · IoT
Project Type0 → 1 Product Design
Discovery & Research

The Problem

Industrial facilities managing Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures relied on paper-based permit-to-work systems prone to errors, missed handoffs, and compliance failures. Without a digital audit trail, organisations faced serious safety and regulatory risk — particularly during shift changes when physical padlock custody transferred between teams.

!Paper-based LOTO permits with no digital audit trail — creating compliance and safety risk
!Shift handoffs for padlock custody were manual, error-prone, and undocumented
!No role-based workflow for Shutdown Directors, WPIs, WPRs, and Task Executors
!Padlock (iFob) inventory status invisible — no real-time view of available vs in-use vs assigned
!Bulk user onboarding required IT involvement — no self-service admin tooling

Objective

Design a role-aware, safety-critical web platform that digitises the full LOTO permit-to-work lifecycle — from lockout creation and padlock assignment through permit issuance, shift transfer, and sign-off — with a full digital audit trail.

Design Goals

✓Role-scoped dashboards: Shutdown Director, WPI, WPR, Task Executor, System Admin
✓Real-time iFob status (Available / In Use / Selected) with cabinet filtering
✓Digital shift transfer with full permit history and handoff documentation
✓Bulk user account creation for large industrial teams
✓Safety-first UX: clear status indicators, confirmation gates, error prevention

Discovery & Research

The Problem

Investors and procurement teams had no reliable, structured way to evaluate the trustworthiness of SMEs before entering business relationships. Existing due diligence processes were manual, inconsistent, and slow — creating significant financial and reputational risk across portfolios.

!No single platform to assess company reliability across financial, legal, and operational dimensions
!Portfolio managers lacked a real-time view of fund allocation across geographies and industries
!Company reports were fragmented across multiple tools — no unified executive summary
!Reliability scoring had no visual hierarchy — users couldn't quickly compare Bronze vs Gold vs Platinum companies

Objective

Design a comprehensive B2B intelligence platform that aggregates company data, generates reliability scores (0–1000), and enables portfolio-level decision-making through dashboards, treemap visualisations, and detailed company reports.

Design Goals

✓Multi-dimensional reliability scoring (Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze) with visual tier badges
✓Portfolio dashboard with fund allocation treemap across geographies and sectors
✓Company detail reports — Executive Summary, Financial Analysis, Reliability Report
✓Sector and category drilldowns: Top Rated, Rising Stars, Newly Listed, Risk Companies
